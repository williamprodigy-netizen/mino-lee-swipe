What matters

The thank-you page is the conversion event, not the opt-in

There is no confirmation message, no calendar link, no “check your email” reassurance. The page says “URGENT! Friend, your spot is almost saved…” with a progress bar at 67% and exactly one button. They manufacture an incomplete-task feeling on a task that was already complete, and spend it on getting the survey filled. Meta's CompleteRegistration fires here, not on the form.

The qualification rule is money x urgency, and it is sitting in public JavaScript

From survey/page-bf93c16709583769.js: you get the call if (priority = “all-in ASAP” OR “top 3”) AND income ≥ $5K/mo, or if income $1K–$5K AND priority = all-in. Everything else redirects to /ai. Someone earning $0–$1K/month never reaches a call, no matter how motivated they say they are. Confirmed live: answering 0–1K followers / all-in / student / $0–1K bounced straight to the SaaS page.

Three of the five survey questions do not affect routing at all

Follower count, “what are you looking for help with” and “what best describes you” are collected and fired to PostHog, but the branch expression only reads priority and income. The other three are closer prep and audience-building data. Worth noticing: they ask the flattering questions first and the money question last, after proof.

Proof is injected mid-survey, immediately before the income question

Between question two and question three the survey stops and shows three income screenshots — Alfonso $14,000 in one month at 172 followers, Richard Lin $17,000 in one month, Thomas Tran $40,000+ in one month. Then it asks what you currently earn. They anchor the number before they measure it.

The consolation prize is a checkout

The registration page promises “free access to our Viral Hooks Writer — $97 FREE” as a show-up bonus. Fail the survey and you land on /ai, which is a three-tier subscription page: $20, $97 and $247 per month. The people who cannot afford the program become recurring revenue instead of a nurture sequence.

Two funnels, one subdomain, one pixel - and they say so in their own code

The source comment reads: “Pixel 829859546688723 — same pixel contentmba-web uses, one pixel across both funnels.” start.contentacademy.io/class is the class funnel; start.contentacademy.io/ is a direct book-a-call page. The qualified thank-you page (/class/call) is byte-identical to the root page — one asset, two entry points.

The countdown is real, and so is the no-replay policy

The FAQ states: “There will be NO replays, so try your best to make it — we'll be giving away bonus free tools/assets for everyone who watches live.” The timer resolves to a genuine fixed datetime (Thu 6:00 PM ET) rather than a rolling evergreen clock. Most competitors in this swipe file fake this. He does not.

Their proof is screenshots with dates on them, not typed claims

The registration page carries a Whop revenue screenshot labelled “WHOP · JAN 1–31, 2025 · GROSS $101,698.55”, four account cards with follower counts (525K / 280K / 180K / 900K), and an aggregate “2.6 BILLION VIEWS” counter. Platform-named, date-stamped, exact to the cent. That is a harder proof format than a testimonial quote.

The VSL is a free download and it contains the whole pitch

9m 51s, Wistia g3is6j1p25, exposed at 4K and 1080p with no token. Pulled with one curl. It names the mechanism, the four stages, five named students, the coach roster, and closes on “book a demo call below” — without ever naming a price.

The offer is a community with named coaches, not a course

The VSL sells three coaches by name and by number: Gabe (monetisation, 0 → $100K/mo agency in a year), Prosper (3,000 → 370,000 followers in a year), Sam (1,000 → 100,000 followers in 30 days, trains UGC creators). The differentiator he leans on is live weekly calls with people who have done it, not recorded modules.

Numbers do not reconcile across their own assets

CAUTION The registration page says “over 60 students” reached 10K. The VSL says he has “coached over 1,500 creators”. The ContentHooks page says “trusted by 10,000+ members” and Whop says 30,820 joined. The follower figures also drift — the class page lists 525K/280K/180K/900K, the ContentHooks page lists 520K/280K/150K/900K for the same four accounts. Do not repeat any single one of these as a fact about their business.

No ad-library coverage

GAP Meta Ad Library returns 403 to this environment and gethookd has no brand record, so creative volume, spend and survival rate are unmeasured. The only paid-traffic evidence is the ?el=ad parameter on the URL Will was served, plus three confirmed trackers.

The class itself was never seen, and could not be

HARD LIMIT The opt-in modal requires a phone number. No research phone number exists, and fabricating one routes a real sales call to a real stranger. So the opt-in was never submitted. The live room, the deck, the pitch, the price, the email sequence and the SMS sequence are all unobserved. Everything on this site is pre-registration evidence only.
